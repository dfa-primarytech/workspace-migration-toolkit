Workspace Migration Toolkit

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

The Workspace Migration Toolkit converts PowerPoint, Publisher, Word and Excel files into Google Slides, Docs and Sheets in your own Google Account. It is run by Primary Technologies Ltd for the schools it supports. This page explains what it does with your information.

What the app can see

You sign in with your Google account and choose which permissions to give. The app asks for:

  • Files you choose, and files it makes (Google Drive drive.file). The app can open only the files you pick, and the files and folders it creates for you. It can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • Adding macros to a Sheet it made (Apps Script script.projects, optional). When a converted Excel workbook had macros, the app puts them into the new Sheet as Google Sheets macros. Without this permission, the macros are left in your folder to add yourself.
  • Your email address (openid email). This is used only to check, at sign-in, that you're using a staff account at a school allowed to use the app. It isn't stored.

Your files

  • A file you convert is processed straight away, in temporary storage on Google Cloud in London (europe-west2). It's deleted as soon as the conversion finishes, whether it succeeded or not.
  • For a Publisher file, each picture is copied for a few minutes to private storage in London, so Google Slides can fetch it through a link that works for 15 minutes. Each copy is deleted once the slide is built, and anything left behind is removed within a day.
  • What the app makes goes into your own Google Drive: a folder holding the converted file, a copy of your original, and a short report. The report lists counts and checks only, never your file's text. For a workbook with macros, the folder also holds the macro code as text files. You own all of these, and you can delete them whenever you like.
  • Your files and their contents are never sent to an AI or language model.

What the app keeps

  • The app has no database and no user accounts. While you're signed in, an encrypted cookie in your browser holds your Google sign-in for up to an hour. Signing out removes it.
  • The app's own logs record a job number, the kind of file, how long it took and any error code. They never record file names, file contents or your email address.
  • Google Cloud keeps standard request logs (such as the time, the page asked for and the IP address) for up to 30 days. The sign-in return address, which carries a one-time sign-in code, is kept out of those logs.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your information is used only to convert the files you choose and to put the results in your Google Account. It isn't sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. The only exception is Google itself, which hosts the app and provides the Drive, Slides, Docs, Sheets and Apps Script services it uses.

Removing the app's access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time, at myaccount.google.com/connections. The files it made stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Questions

Ask your school's IT support, who can pass your question to Primary Technologies Ltd.
